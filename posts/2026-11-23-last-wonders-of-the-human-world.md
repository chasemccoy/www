Like the rest of the internet I’ve been captured by the recent release of GTA 6. 

The scale, the sheer breadth and depth of attention paid as part of developing this game over the course of the past decade may very well represent one of the last great creative achievements driven largely by human minds.

It's plausible to assume that some generative AI was used in GTA 6's development, but the game was largely built before those tools were ubiquitous. There may never be a game of its scale developed without AI again.
